# SDG 13: Climate Action — official UN targets

- **Source:** United Nations, Sustainable Development Goals knowledge platform
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal13
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- Target 13.1 calls to "strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to
  climate-related hazards and natural disasters in all countries."
- Disaster-related deaths dropped **65%**, from **2.11 per 100,000** people
  (2005–2014) to **0.75** (2015–2024).
- Over that same shift, people *affected* by disasters roughly doubled, from
  **1,199 to 2,445 per 100,000** population.
- **141 countries** had adopted national disaster risk reduction strategies
  by 2025.
- Global temperatures in 2025 were **1.43°C above pre-industrial levels**.
- Target 13.3 targets "education, awareness-raising and human and
  institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact
  reduction and early warning" — and **47%** of national curriculum
  frameworks still don't mention climate change at all.
- Target 13.a: developed countries committed to mobilize **$100 billion
  annually by 2020**; COP29 (2024) raised that to a goal of **$1.3 trillion
  annually by 2035**.

## Direct quotes

> "Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to climate-related hazards and

## The targets, by number

- **13.1** — resilience and adaptive capacity to climate hazards and
  disasters
- **13.2** — integrate climate measures into national policies and planning
- **13.3** — education, awareness, and institutional capacity, including
  early warning
- **13.a** — mobilize climate finance for developing countries
- **13.b** — build capacity for climate planning in least-developed countries
  and small island states

## Where this project fits

Extreme heat is a "climate-related hazard" under 13.1, and a neighborhood
early-warning or cooling program is exactly the kind of "adaptive capacity"
and "early warning" work named in 13.1 and 13.3. This page states the
targets only — it has no city- or country-level heat figures, so it can't be
used as evidence for a specific death count or region.

## Summary

The UN's own SDG 13 targets frame climate action around resilience, policy
integration, education and early warning, and finance — with global
indicators (disaster deaths down 65%, people affected roughly doubled) that
say the world is getting better at surviving disasters but worse at avoiding
being exposed to them in the first place. Nothing on this page is specific
to heat, Los Angeles, or South Korea.
