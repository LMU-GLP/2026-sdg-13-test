# 2 in 3 people in heat-related deaths aged over 60: KDCA

- **URL:** https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/environment-animals/20250709/2-in-3-people-in-heat-related-deaths-aged-over-60-according-to-kdca
- **Accessed:** 2026-07-29
- **Region:** South Korea
- **Type:** news

By Yonhap via The Korea Times, published July 9, 2025.

## Key facts

- Cumulative toll: "238 people have died from heat-related illnesses since
  the government started monitoring such deaths in 2011" (Korea Disease
  Control and Prevention Agency data).
- Elderly share: of those 238 deaths, "156 people, or over 65 percent,
  were aged 60 years or older."
- 2025 season (as of July 8): seven deaths recorded, four of the victims
  aged 70 or older.
- Where people were stricken: farms 32% of heat-related deaths, homes 15%,
  near roads approximately 14%.
- Recent cases cited: a woman in her 80s died near a greenhouse in Jinju
  (280 kilometers south of Seoul); another woman in her 80s was found dead
  in a rice paddy on Jeju Island with second-degree burns.
- KDCA guidance: agricultural workers should "work in early mornings or
  late evenings and avoid going outdoors in the hours between noon and
  5 p.m."

## Summary

KDCA figures reported by Yonhap show South Korea's heat deaths fall
overwhelmingly on the old: more than 65 percent of the 238 people killed
by heat illness since monitoring began in 2011 were 60 or older, and four
of the seven deaths so far in 2025 were people in their 70s or above.
Roughly a third of victims were stricken on farms and another 15 percent
in their own homes, with the profiled cases being women in their 80s who
died doing agricultural work in extreme heat.
