# 온열질환 사망자 70%는 고령자·장애인…예방매뉴얼만으로는 한계 (70% of heat-illness deaths are elderly or disabled — prevention manuals alone aren't enough)

- **Source:** Medical World News, citing data from Rep. So Byung-hoon
  (Democratic Party, Gyeonggi Gwangju) and the Korea Disease Control and
  Prevention Agency (질병관리청), published October 14, 2025
- **URL:** https://medicalworldnews.co.kr/m/view.php?idx=1510970550
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- As of mid-September 2025, that season's heat-related illness deaths
  totaled **29**: "2025년 9월 중순 기준 온열질환 사망자는 29명."
- Of those, **17 (59%) were elderly**: "이 중 고령자가 17명(59%)."
- **3 (10%) were people with disabilities**: "장애인이 3명(10%)."
- Combined, vulnerable groups (elderly + disabled) were about **70%** of
  deaths: "전체의 약 70%가 취약계층이었다."
- The figure that closes our gap: **5 elderly people died specifically
  while doing outdoor work in fields**: "특히 논밭 등 실외작업 중 사망한
  고령자가 5명에 달해." That's 5 of 29 deaths (about 17%) that are, in this
  season's count, both elderly *and* agricultural.

## This is a different window than our other Korea source

[korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md](korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md) reports a
13-year cumulative total (238 deaths, 2011–2024) with separate percentages
for age (65%+ are 60+) and setting (32% on farms), but never states the
overlap between the two. This article covers a single season (through
mid-September 2025, 29 deaths) but is the only source found so far that
directly names an elderly-and-farm-work intersection: 5 of 29. The two
sources should not be merged into one figure — different time windows,
different totals — but read together they support the same shape of
problem from two separate counts.

## Summary

In a legislator's citation of KDCA data for the 2025 heat season through
mid-September, 29 people had died of heat illness, 59% of them elderly and
70% elderly-or-disabled combined, and of those, 5 were elderly people who
died specifically doing outdoor farm work — the clearest single-source
figure yet for the elderly-agricultural-worker overlap this wiki was
missing.
