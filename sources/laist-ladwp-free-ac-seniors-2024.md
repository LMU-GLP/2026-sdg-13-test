# Free AC for LA seniors

- **URL:** https://laist.com/brief/news/climate-environment/la-giving-out-free-ac-units-how-to-get-one
- **Accessed:** 2026-07-29
- **Region:** Los Angeles
- **Type:** program

By Erin Stone, LAist, published September 6, 2024. Covers the free
air-conditioner giveaway for seniors run by the LA Department of Water
and Power (LADWP) with the City's Department of Aging.

## Key facts

- Program: LADWP and the City's Department of Aging distribute free
  portable air-conditioning units to seniors.
- Eligibility: age 60 or older, fixed low income, lacking sufficient air
  conditioning, and enrolled in or qualified for LADWP's EZ-SAVE or
  Lifeline discount rate programs.
- Scale at the covered event: approximately 400 portable AC units,
  distributed first-come at the Lincoln Park Senior Center in Lincoln
  Heights (September 9, 2024), photo ID and an LADWP bill required.
- Season total: LADWP had distributed over 500 units across multiple
  events that summer, with prior giveaways drawing 300–400 attendees
  each.
- Rationale: older adults face heightened heat risk because, per the CDC,
  "they're more likely to have chronic health issues or take medications
  that affect the body's response to heat."

## Summary

Los Angeles already runs a targeted intervention for our exact focus
population: free portable AC units for low-income residents 60 and older,
distributed by LADWP and the Department of Aging at senior-center events.
The scale is small — hundreds of units per summer in a county whose own
data brief counts thousands of heat-illness ER visits — and it requires
seniors to have utility-account paperwork and physically show up, which
is itself a gap worth probing.
