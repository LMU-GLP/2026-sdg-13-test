# Existing programs: who's already working on this

Before pitching anything new, this page is the check: what's already
running, in either candidate region or elsewhere.

## Los Angeles County

**Source:** [la-extreme-heat.md](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)

The county already runs: heat alerts by text and email subscription, a
schools toolkit, a Heat Ambassador community-health-worker initiative
(funded by the LARC-HEAT grant), and a County Heat Action Plan
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)). The source names these programs
but does not include any evaluation of how many illnesses or deaths they've
prevented — that's a gap (see
[Gaps and open questions](gaps-and-open-questions.md)).

## Ahmedabad, India: Heat Action Plan

**Source:** [ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md](../sources/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md)

Launched in **2013** — the first Heat Action Plan in India and South Asia —
after a 2010 heat wave whose true toll ("more than **1,300**" excess
deaths) turned out to be far higher than the roughly-a-dozen deaths locals
initially believed occurred
([source](../sources/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md)).

Four parts, per the source:

1. Color-coded early warning, up to **5 days'** notice before a heat wave.
2. Multilingual community outreach — pamphlets, billboards, radio, social
   media.
3. Healthcare provider training, from administrators to community health
   workers.
4. Reflective ("cool") roof painting to lower indoor heat without air
   conditioning.

Early research credits it with "averting approximately **1,190 deaths each
year**" since launch ([source](../sources/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md)).

**This is the only program in this wiki with an independent mortality
evaluation behind it.** Neither the LA County programs nor (as far as this
wiki's sources show) any South Korea program have a comparable evaluation.

## Do not compare Ahmedabad's 1,190 to LA's or Korea's death counts

1,190 is an estimate of deaths *avoided* by an intervention in one Indian
city with different density, climate, and AC access than LA or Korea — not
a recorded death count, and not transferable as "how many lives this would
save elsewhere" ([source](../sources/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md)).

## South Korea

No program, warning system, or organized outreach effort is named in this
wiki's Korea source ([korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)) —
only official advice to avoid outdoor work at midday. If the team leans
toward Korea as a region, finding a source on an actual Korean program
(national or local) is the next research gap to close.
