# Research checklist

Open questions, phrased as source-hunting tasks. **Teammates: claim an item by putting your
name after it**, find 1–3 sources, and add them to `sources/` (in a solo session: "add this
source as a markdown file in sources/, don't edit index.md or the wiki yet"). Check items
off only after the source is ingested into the wiki.

## Impact data ([topic page](topics/impact-data.md))

- [ ] LA County heat deaths / heat-related ER visits, and the elderly share — official counts *and* an excess-mortality estimate
- [ ] Korea KDCA heat-illness surveillance: latest summer's cases and deaths, elderly share
- [ ] Temperature trends: extreme-heat days per year, both regions, then vs. now
- [ ] Neighborhood-level disparity: hottest LA neighborhoods vs. city average; hottest Seoul districts or rural counties

## Existing programs ([topic page](topics/existing-programs.md))

- [ ] LA cooling centers: how many, where, and any usage/utilization numbers
- [ ] LA heat alerts + Chief Heat Officer / heat action planning: what's actually implemented
- [ ] Korea cooling shelters (무더위쉼터) and elderly check-in programs: scale and reported effectiveness
- [ ] One strong precedent from elsewhere (e.g. Paris CHALEX registry, Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan)

## Lived experience ([topic page](topics/lived-experience.md))

- [ ] Reporting or research on *why* seniors don't use cooling centers/shelters (either region)
- [ ] At least one profile/story of an elderly resident during a heatwave, per region
- [ ] Who elderly residents trust and respond to (family, neighbors, officials, nurses)

## Root causes ([topic page](topics/root-causes.md))

- [ ] LA urban heat island + redlining/tree-canopy evidence
- [ ] Korea housing risk: banjiha/jjokbang or rural elderly-farmer exposure
- [ ] Energy poverty: seniors rationing AC due to cost (either region)
- [ ] Social isolation as a heat-death risk factor (Chicago 1995 / Europe 2003 literature is fine)

## Deciding the region ([comparison page](region-comparison.md))

- [ ] A nameable candidate neighborhood in LA with senior demographics
- [ ] A nameable candidate neighborhood/county in Korea with senior demographics
