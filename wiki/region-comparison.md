## Emerging picture (not a verdict)

- **Different death profiles.** Korea's documented elderly heat deaths
  skew toward outdoor agricultural work (farms 32% of deaths); LA's
  danger concentrates indoors, in homes without AC, from about 85°F
  sustained ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md);
  [ABC7, 2024](../sources/abc7-socal-heat-deaths-undercounted-2024.md)).
  An intervention would look different in each region.
- **Korea has the sharper elderly statistic; LA has the bigger hidden
  toll.** Korea: 2 in 3 deaths are 60+. LA: excess-death estimates run
  roughly ten times the official count.
- **Evidence imbalance to fix before deciding:** LA holds 4 of our 5
  sources (news, dataset, program). Korea has one national news source —
  no dataset, no program source, nothing Seoul-specific. See
  [research-plan.md](research-plan.md).
