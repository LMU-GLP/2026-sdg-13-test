## Harm (decision test 1)

- National toll: "238 people have died from heat-related illnesses since
  the government started monitoring such deaths in 2011" (KDCA data)
  ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
- The deaths are overwhelmingly elderly: "156 people, or over 65
  percent, were aged 60 years or older"
  ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
  This is the single sharpest elderly-harm statistic in our wiki so far.
- 2025 season (as of July 8): seven deaths, four of them aged 70 or
  older ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
- Where victims are stricken: farms 32%, homes 15%, near roads ~14% —
  the profiled cases are women in their 80s who died doing agricultural
  work in extreme heat (one near a greenhouse in Jinju, one in a Jeju
  rice paddy)
  ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
- Risk profile differs from LA's: Korea's documented elderly heat deaths
  skew toward **outdoor work** (farms), while LA's danger concentrates
  **indoors** in homes without AC — see
  [region-comparison.md](region-comparison.md).

## Existing responses and their gaps (decision test 2)

- KDCA issues behavioral guidance — agricultural workers should "work in
  early mornings or late evenings and avoid going outdoors in the hours
  between noon and 5 p.m."
  ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
  That people keep dying in fields at those hours suggests guidance
  alone isn't protective. *(inference from the cited deaths — treat as a
  hypothesis to verify)*
- The heat wave alert system (KMA), the cooling-shelter network
  (무더위쉼터), and check-in programs for isolated seniors are known to
  exist but are **not yet covered by any source in this wiki**.
  *(uncited)* — logged in [research-plan.md](research-plan.md).

## Data availability (decision test 3)

- National surveillance is solid and age-disaggregated: KDCA has
  monitored heat deaths since 2011 and reports age breakdowns and
  location-of-incident shares
  ([Korea Times/Yonhap, 2025](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
- **Gap:** everything we hold is national. We have no Seoul-city or
  neighborhood (dong)-level source yet, and the farm-heavy death profile
  may describe rural Korea better than Seoul itself. *(uncited)*

## Still open

- Seoul-specific numbers: deaths, ER visits, and the elderly share
  within the city (e.g. the record 2018 summer, when heat killed a
  record number nationwide).
- Coverage and actual use of cooling shelters by elderly Seoulites.
- Housing exposure: elderly residents of semi-basement (banjiha) and
  rooftop units.
