## What we need to establish

### Harm (decision test 1)
- What do Korea's heat illness surveillance and mortality statistics show,
  and what share of victims are elderly? How bad were the record summers of
  recent years?
- Where do at-risk elderly people live and work — e.g. elderly residents of
  semi-basement (banjiha) or rooftop housing, and elderly outdoor workers
  such as farmers and cardboard collectors?
- How does Korea's rapid population aging change the trajectory of risk?

### Existing responses and their gaps (decision test 2)
- The national heat wave alert system (KMA) and the "cooling shelter"
  (무더위쉼터) network: coverage, actual use by elderly people, known
  failures.
- Programs targeting isolated seniors (visiting care workers, smart-plug /
  phone check-in pilots): what exists, what's the evidence it works?

### Data availability (decision test 3)
- KDCA heat-related illness reports, KMA climate data, Seoul city open
  data. How much is available at neighborhood (dong) level, and how much is
  accessible in English?
