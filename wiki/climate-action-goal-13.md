# SDG Goal 13 and Our Challenge

Our challenge — **how might we help a neighborhood stay safe during extreme
heat?** — sits inside UN Sustainable Development Goal 13, "Climate Action."
Full target list: [UN SDG Goal 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md).

## The goal statement

"Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts"
([UN SDG Goal 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).

## Which target actually fits

Goal 13 has no target that names heat, or even extreme weather, directly.
The closest fit is **Target 13.1**: "Strengthen resilience and adaptive
capacity to climate-related hazards and natural disasters in all countries"
([UN SDG Goal 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)). Two of its
indicators matter for a neighborhood-scale project:

- **13.1.1** — "Number of deaths, missing persons and directly affected
  persons attributed to disasters per 100,000 population." This is the
  shape of measurement our two heat-death sources are already producing,
  informally: see
  [LA extreme heat](la-extreme-heat.md) and
  [South Korea extreme heat](south-korea-extreme-heat.md).
- **13.1.3** — "Proportion of local governments that adopt and implement
  local disaster risk reduction strategies in line with national disaster
  risk reduction strategies." This is the neighborhood/city scale the
  challenge is asking about, not the national scale most of Goal 13's other
  indicators track.

Targets 13.2 (national climate policy integration, greenhouse gas
reporting) and 13.3 (climate education mainstreaming) operate at the
national level and don't have an obvious neighborhood-scale analogue.
Targets 13.a and 13.b are about financing for developing countries and
small island states and don't apply to a single-neighborhood project
([UN SDG Goal 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).

## What this means for scoping the project

A neighborhood heat-safety project is best framed against 13.1: it's
building *local* resilience and adaptive capacity to a *climate-related
hazard*. The UN source itself supplies no heat-specific figures or
funding numbers — for those, see
[LA extreme heat](la-extreme-heat.md),
[South Korea extreme heat](south-korea-extreme-heat.md), and
[heat-death undercounting](heat-death-undercounting.md).
