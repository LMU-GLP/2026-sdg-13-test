# National U.S. heat mortality trends

**Source behind this page:**
[cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md](../sources/cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md)
("Trends in Rates of Heat-Related Deaths Across Population Groups in the
United States, 2000-2023," peer-reviewed, PubMed Central)

## The trend

Heat-related death rates rose for every population group tracked in the
U.S. between 2000 and 2023, and the study describes "increasing mortality
rates across all groups, with steep rises since 2018"
([source](../sources/cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md)).

## By group (rate per 100,000, not raw counts — see note below)

- **American Indian/Alaska Native:** highest of any group, 2–3x other
  groups. 1.07 (2011) → 2.51 (2023). Average annual increase 8.7%
  (2011–2023), accelerating to 27.8%/year (2018–2023).
- **Hispanic:** 0.18 (2000) → 0.76 (2023). 28.7%/year increase (2019–2023).
- **Non-Hispanic Black:** 0.50 (2000) → 0.94 (2023). 28.6%/year increase
  (2019–2023).
- **Non-Hispanic White:** 0.14 (2000) → 0.55 (2023). 23.9%/year increase
  (2019–2023).
- **Asian American/Pacific Islander:** most modest historical increase;
  25.2%/year (2020–2023).

(All figures: [source](../sources/cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md))

## Raw 2023 death counts, for scale

1,408 non-Hispanic White; 411 Hispanic; 407 non-Hispanic Black; 62 American
Indian/Alaska Native; 42 Asian American/Pacific Islander
([source](../sources/cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md)).

**Don't average the percentage changes across groups** — they're rates
within groups of very different sizes, not shares of one total
([source](../sources/cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md)).

## This is national, not LA or Korea

This dataset cannot be merged with the LA County or South Korea figures —
different geography, time window, and unit (national rate per 100,000 by
race/ethnicity, vs. county death counts, vs. a national-but-different-country
count by age). It's useful only as context: the ~24–29%-a-year acceleration
seen nationally since about 2018–2019 in nearly every group matches the
direction LA County's own numbers show locally (see
[Los Angeles](los-angeles.md)).
