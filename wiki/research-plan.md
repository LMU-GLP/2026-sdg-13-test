## Highest-value next sources

Ranked by what the decision test still can't answer — note the current
evidence imbalance (4 of 5 sources are LA-side or global; Korea has one):

1. **Korea dataset or program source.** KDCA annual heat-illness
   surveillance report, or coverage of the cooling-shelter network
   (무더위쉼터) / smart-plug check-in pilots for isolated seniors.
   Without it, comparison row 2 is uncited on the Korea side.
2. **Seoul-city-specific harm data.** E.g. the record 2018 summer (Seoul
   hit 39.6°C); dong-level or city-level deaths and ER figures. Our
   Korea numbers are national and farm-heavy, which may not describe
   Seoul.
3. **LA elderly share of heat deaths/ER visits.** We know 65+ is
   high-risk but hold no LA age-share number to match Korea's "2 in 3."
4. **Alert reach among elderly people.** Any evidence on whether opt-in
   heat alerts (LA) or KMA warnings (Korea) actually reach isolated
   seniors — this decides the "hardest to reach" hypothesis in
   [challenge.md](challenge.md).

## Open questions

1. ~~In each region, how many elderly people are harmed by heat?~~
   Partially answered: Korea nationally, yes ("2 in 3" deaths 60+); LA
   still lacks an elderly-share figure. See region pages.
2. What already exists to protect them, and where does it fail? LA:
   partially answered (senior AC giveaway too small, opt-in alerts).
   Korea: only behavioral guidance sourced so far.
3. How local and citable is the data? LA: county-level yes,
   neighborhood partial. Korea: national only.
4. Which solution direction does the evidence point toward? Still open —
   note the regions may demand different ones (indoor cooling in LA vs
   outdoor-work protection in rural Korea; Seoul profile unknown). See
   [region-comparison.md](region-comparison.md).

## Known gaps

- No Korea dataset or program source; nothing Seoul-specific.
- No LA number for the elderly share of heat deaths or ER visits.
- No evidence yet on alert reach / cooling-center use by elderly people
  in either region.
