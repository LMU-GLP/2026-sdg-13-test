## Open questions

Keyed to the decision test. Detailed per-region versions live in
[los-angeles.md](los-angeles.md) and [seoul.md](seoul.md).

1. In each region, how many elderly people are harmed by heat each summer,
   and how confident are those numbers?
2. In each region, what already exists to protect them — and where does it
   demonstrably fail to reach isolated elderly people?
3. In each region, how local and citable is the available data?
4. Which candidate solution direction (check-in networks, cooling access,
   activating alerts) does the evidence point toward?
