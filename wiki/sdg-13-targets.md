# SDG 13 targets, and how this challenge maps to them

**Source behind this page:**
[un-sdg13-targets.md](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md) (UN Sustainable
Development Goals platform)

## The five targets

- **13.1** — "Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to
  climate-related hazards and natural disasters in all countries"
  ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).
- **13.2** — integrate climate change measures into national policies,
  strategies and planning ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).
- **13.3** — "Improve education, awareness-raising and human and
  institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact
  reduction and early warning" ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).
- **13.a** — mobilize climate finance for developing countries; COP29
  (2024) set a goal of **$1.3 trillion annually by 2035**
  ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).
- **13.b** — build climate-planning capacity in least-developed countries
  and small island states ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).

## Global indicators worth knowing

- Disaster-related deaths dropped **65%**, from **2.11 per 100,000** people
  (2005–2014) to **0.75** (2015–2024) — worldwide, not heat-specific
  ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).
- Over the same shift, people *affected* by disasters roughly doubled, from
  **1,199 to 2,445 per 100,000** ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).
- **47%** of national curriculum frameworks still don't mention climate
  change ([source](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).

These are global, all-hazard figures — the source doesn't break them down by
hazard type or by country, so they can't be used to claim anything
heat-specific about LA or Korea.

## Where a neighborhood heat project lands

A neighborhood-level early-warning system, cooling-center network, or
outreach campaign is squarely a Target 13.1 and 13.3 project: it builds
"adaptive capacity" to a "climate-related hazard" (heat) through "early
warning" and "awareness-raising." It is not a 13.a or 13.b project unless
the pitch specifically involves financing a developing country's capacity —
worth deciding explicitly rather than by default.
