# South Korea: extreme heat

**Source behind this page:**
[korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)
(Yonhap via *The Korea Times*, July 9, 2025, citing the Korea Disease
Control and Prevention Agency / KDCA)

## The headline figure

**238 people have died of heat-related illness** in South Korea since KDCA
monitoring began in 2011. **156 of them — over 65% — were aged 60 or older**
([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)).

As of July 8, 2025, that year alone had seven deaths, four of them aged 70
or older ([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)).

## Where it happens

Of recorded deaths: **32% on farms**, **15% at home**, roughly **14% near
roads** ([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)).

## The shape of the problem

The source names two specific 2025 cases: a woman in her 80s found
unconscious near a greenhouse in Jinju, and another woman in her 80s found
dead in a rice paddy on Jeju Island with second-degree burns
([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)). Both were elderly women
doing agricultural work alone. Combined with the 32% farm-death figure, the
pattern this source documents is elderly people working outdoors, often
alone, in agriculture.

## The official advice

Health authorities "recommended agriculture workers to work in early
mornings or late evenings and avoid going outdoors in the hours between noon
and 5 p.m." ([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)) — advice
that assumes the worker has someone else available to do the midday work,
which the source itself flags as a gap for people who don't.

## What this source does not cover

No government program, warning system, or cooling-center initiative is
named in this source. This wiki does not yet have a Korea-specific example
