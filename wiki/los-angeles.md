# Los Angeles County: extreme heat

**Source behind this page:** [la-extreme-heat.md](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)
(LA County Department of Public Health, Office of Environmental Justice and
Climate Health, November 2025 data brief)

## The hazard is growing

LA County counts an "extreme heat day" as a downtown maximum of **90°F or
higher**. There were **509 of them between 2005 and 2024**
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)). The heat season itself — first
heat wave to last — is **46 days longer than it was in the 1960s**
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).

## Health impact

- ER visits for heat-related illness, 2005–2022, averaged **898 a year**,
  ranging from a low of 456 (2005) to a high of **1,352 (2018)**; **1,195**
  in 2022. July produces the most ER visits of any month (**4,020** across
  2005–2022), even though later months have more extreme-heat days —
  according to the brief, early-season heat hits bodies before they've
  acclimatized ([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).
- Hospital admissions over the same period averaged **196 a year**, from 96
  (2011) to 461 (2018) ([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).
- **125 heat deaths were officially recorded from 2015–2024**, peaking at 22
  (2018), 21 (2020), and 22 (2022) — compared with just **34** across the
  entire prior decade, 2005–2014 ([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).

## The county's own numbers don't agree with each other

LA County's data brief states its own official death count is likely far
too low: a 2024 Medical Examiner study found "elevated heat may have led to
as many as **1,265 additional deaths** between 2014–2019"
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)) — against **125** officially
recorded deaths for the longer 2015–2024 window. That's roughly an
order-of-magnitude gap, in the county's own analysis, because (per the
brief) heat usually kills indirectly, by worsening a condition like heart
failure, so a death certificate rarely names heat as the cause
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).

The same study found deaths rose **7%, 15%, and 66%** on days of moderate,
major, and extreme HeatRisk respectively, compared with no-risk days
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).

## Who's most at risk

The brief names older adults among the highest-risk groups
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)). No source in this wiki yet gives
an LA-specific breakdown by age with hard numbers the way the Korea source
does — that's a gap (see [Gaps and open questions](gaps-and-open-questions.md)).

## What the county already runs

Heat alerts by text and email subscription, a schools toolkit, a Heat
Ambassador community-health-worker initiative (LARC-HEAT grant funded), and
a County Heat Action Plan ([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)). See
[Existing programs](existing-programs.md) for more, including a comparison
point: Ahmedabad's Heat Action Plan has an independent mortality evaluation
behind it; this wiki's source does not say whether LA's plan does.
