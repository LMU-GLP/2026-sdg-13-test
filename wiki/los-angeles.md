## What we need to establish

### Harm (decision test 1)
- How many heat-related deaths and ER visits does LA County record, and how
  are they undercounted? What share are elderly residents?
- Which neighborhoods run hottest (urban heat island), and do they overlap
  with where low-income elderly people live?
- How common is it for elderly Angelenos to lack air conditioning or to
  avoid running it because of cost?

### Existing responses and their gaps (decision test 2)
- Cooling centers: where are they, who actually shows up, and can elderly
  residents physically get to them?
- Heat alerts (NWS excessive heat warnings, county emergency alerts): do
  they reach and activate isolated elderly people?
- Any check-in programs for isolated seniors during heat waves?

### Data availability (decision test 3)
- LA County Department of Public Health heat reports; CalEPA / CDC heat
  vulnerability maps; UCLA heat research. How fine-grained does the public
  data get?
