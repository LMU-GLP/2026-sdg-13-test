## Why LA is a candidate

- Extreme heat is LA County's deadliest climate hazard; heat deaths are widely believed to
  be undercounted relative to official records. *[unsourced]*
- Heat burden is very unevenly distributed: inland valleys (San Fernando Valley — e.g.
  Pacoima, Sun Valley — and the San Gabriel Valley) run far hotter than coastal areas, and
  historically redlined, low-canopy neighborhoods are hottest of all. *[unsourced]*
- Many older homes in the hottest neighborhoods lack central AC; some elderly residents own
  units but restrict use because of electricity cost. *[unsourced]*

## What exists already (verify and detail in [existing programs](../topics/existing-programs.md))

- County/city cooling centers activated during heat emergencies; utilization is reported to
  be low. *[unsourced]*
- NWS heat watches/warnings; a county emergency alert system. *[unsourced]*
- City/county Climate Emergency Mobilization Office and a Chief Heat Officer role have
  driven recent heat planning. *[unsourced]*
