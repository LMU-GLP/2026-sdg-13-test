## Why South Korea is a candidate

- Korea runs a national real-time heat-illness surveillance system (KDCA) that publishes
  counts of heat illness cases and deaths each summer — unusually good data. *[unsourced]*
- Korea is one of the fastest-aging societies in the world; elderly people working outdoors
  (notably older farmers in rural counties) are repeatedly reported among heat fatalities.
  *[unsourced]*
- Recent summers (including 2018 and the mid-2020s) set heat records, and the government
  has treated heatwaves as a natural disaster category. *[unsourced]*

## What exists already (verify and detail in [existing programs](../topics/existing-programs.md))

- Tens of thousands of designated cooling shelters (무더위쉼터), often in senior centers
  (경로당), community centers, and banks. *[unsourced]*
- Village broadcast systems and social-worker/visiting-nurse check-ins on vulnerable elderly
  during heat warnings; KMA heat advisories pushed to phones. *[unsourced]*
- Some local governments distribute fans, cooling kits, or electricity subsidies to
  low-income elderly households. *[unsourced]*
