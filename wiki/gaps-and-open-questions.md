# Gaps and open questions

What this wiki cannot yet answer, so nobody mistakes a missing citation for
a settled fact.

## No neighborhood-level data anywhere

LA figures are county-wide ([la-extreme-heat.md](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)).
Korea figures are national ([korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)).
Nothing in this wiki is scoped to an actual neighborhood — whichever region
is chosen, finding a source at that finer grain is the highest-priority next
step.

## No South Korea program source

[Existing programs](existing-programs.md) has a well-evaluated example for
Ahmedabad and a named-but-unevaluated set of programs for LA, but nothing
for South Korea. If the team leans Korea, this is the gap to close before a
"our idea doesn't already exist" claim can be defended.

## LA has no age/risk breakdown as specific as Korea's

Korea's source states 65%+ of deaths are age 60+
([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)). LA's brief names older
adults as high-risk but gives no equivalent percentage
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)) — so any claim that "X% of LA's
heat deaths are elderly" would be invented, not sourced.

## LA has no death-by-setting breakdown

Korea's source can say 32% of deaths happen on farms
([source](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)). LA's brief has no
equivalent (home vs. outdoors vs. workplace)
([source](../sources/la-extreme-heat.md)) — don't assume LA's pattern
mirrors Korea's just because both name heat exposure as the cause.

## The three national/global-vs-local mismatches

Three sources in this wiki are explicitly not comparable to the two
region-specific ones, and citing them as if they were would break this
wiki's own rule against comparing figures that don't measure the same
thing:

- [UN SDG 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md) — global, all-hazard,
  not heat-specific.
- [CDC national heat mortality trends](../sources/cdc-us-heat-mortality-trends.md)
  — U.S.-wide by race/ethnicity, not LA-specific.
- [Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan](../sources/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md) —
  a different city's intervention result, not a baseline for LA or Korea.

See [Los Angeles vs. South Korea](la-vs-south-korea.md) for the full
reasoning on what is and isn't comparable between the two candidate
regions specifically.

## No program-effectiveness evaluation for LA or Korea

Ahmedabad's plan has a peer-reviewed mortality evaluation behind it
([source](../sources/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md)). Nothing in this wiki
evaluates whether LA County's existing programs, or any Korean program,
actually reduce illness or death.
