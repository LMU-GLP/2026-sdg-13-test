## Why elderly people are most at risk in heat

- Physiology: aging reduces the body's ability to sense heat, sweat, and regulate
  temperature; common medications (for blood pressure, heart conditions, mental health)
  further impair heat response. *[unsourced]*
- Chronic illness: heat deaths in older adults often present as cardiovascular or
  respiratory events, which is one reason official heat death counts run low. *[unsourced]*
- Social isolation: living alone is a major risk multiplier — in famous heat disasters
  (Chicago 1995, Europe 2003) isolated elderly people made up most of the dead. *[unsourced]*
- Economics and behavior: fixed incomes make people ration AC; some distrust or dislike
  cooling centers; many underestimate their own risk ("I've handled heat all my life").
  *[unsourced]*
