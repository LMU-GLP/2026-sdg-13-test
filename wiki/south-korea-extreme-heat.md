# Extreme Heat in South Korea

Source: [Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md).

## What the KDCA data shows

Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) figures, reported by
Yonhap via The Korea Times:

- Cumulative toll since monitoring began: "238 people have died from
  heat-related illnesses since the government started monitoring such
  deaths in 2011."
  ([Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md))
- Elderly skew: of those 238 deaths, "156 people, or over 65 percent, were
  aged 60 years or older."
  ([Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md))
- 2025 season (as of July 8, 2025): seven deaths, four of them aged 70 or
  older.
  ([Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md))
- Where deaths happen: farms account for 32% of heat-related deaths, homes
  15%, near roads about 14%.
  ([Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md))
- Two cases named directly: a woman in her 80s died near a greenhouse in
  Jinju; another woman in her 80s was found dead in a rice paddy on Jeju
  Island with second-degree burns.
  ([Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md))

## What's already being recommended

KDCA guidance quoted in the article tells agricultural workers to "work in
early mornings or late evenings and avoid going outdoors in the hours
between noon and 5 p.m."
([Korea Times / KDCA report](../sources/korea-times-heat-deaths-elderly-2025.md)).
This is behavioral guidance, not a funded program or piece of
infrastructure — we have no South Korea source describing something
equivalent to LA's Cool Streets program or Heat Ambassador initiative (see
[interventions and programs](interventions-and-programs.md) for what we do
have).

## Data gap

This page rests on one news article summarizing KDCA figures, not a
government dataset page or brief in the way the LA County DPH source is.
It gives a cumulative count (238 deaths since 2011) rather than an
annual breakdown, which limits any year-over-year trend comparison with
LA's yearly figures — see
[LA vs. South Korea comparison](la-vs-south-korea-comparison.md) for why
that matters.
