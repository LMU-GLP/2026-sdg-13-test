# Lived experience

The human side: why elderly people don't use cooling centers, what a heatwave feels like
in a specific apartment, who notices when someone is struggling. Judges remember one true
story better than ten statistics — and the prototype will live or die on these barriers.

## What we're hunting for

- Reported reasons seniors avoid cooling centers (transport, dignity, pets, routine,
  "it's not that bad", not wanting to be a burden).
- Interviews, ethnographies, and journalism profiling individual elderly residents during
  heat events in either region.
- How heat interacts with daily routines: when do risky hours fall, who is home, what do
  people already do to cope?
- Trust: who do elderly residents actually open the door for / answer the phone for?
